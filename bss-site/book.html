Monograph · Qur'anic Studies

A Beginner's Journey into Understanding Islam

By Shaykh Dr. Tariq Al-Hashimi

Summary

A serene, beginner-friendly introduction to Islam's central beliefs, values, and way of life. Written for thoughtful inquirers and seekers exploring foundational theological tenets and spiritual reflection without polemical debate.

For whom this book is written

  • Seekers desiring a calm, non-polemical overview of the sacred text
  • Students of comparative religion exploring Qur’anic literary structures
  • Readers seeking to understand oral recitation and linguistic symmetry
  • Anyone wanting grounded scholarly context without academic jargon
